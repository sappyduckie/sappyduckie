## Definitions

Wherein throughout this document the following terms are used, they are to be understood by the following definitions.
- **AI**: Systems that can generate content based on probabilistic inference.
- **Agent**: Autonomous systems that have the capacity to perform actions based on internal reasoning with a limited degree of human intervention.

## Contributions

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Contributions, human-made or otherwise, are all held to the same standards of quality. Some contributions can only be made by a human.
